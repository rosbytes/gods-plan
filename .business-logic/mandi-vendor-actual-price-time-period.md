## Mandi Vendor Actual Price Updation Time Period

- **11:01 PM - 4:15 AM**: Mandi Vendors can **only update actual price between 11:01 PM - 4:15 AM** (local time).
- **Cutoff Enforcement**: The system must reject all actual price updation attempt after 4:15 AM.
