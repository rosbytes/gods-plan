## Mandi Vendor Expected Price Updation Cutoff Time

- **7:00 PM**: Mandi Vendors can **only update expected price before 7:00 PM** (local time).
- **Cutoff Enforcement**: The system must reject all expected price updation attempt after 7:01 PM.
